Come to the water. Know its story. Stay a while.
  1. Click Copy signature. If your browser blocks it, the signature is selected for you: press Cmd+C (Mac) or Ctrl+C (Windows).
  2. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, then the General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature, click Create new, name it Dock, and paste into the box.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose Dock for hello@thedock.life, for new emails and for replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
